Crapless Craps - Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 10, 2026

Thank you for playing Crapless Craps ("the App"). Your privacy is very important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected, how it is used, and how your privacy rights are protected when you use our application.

Key Summary: Crapless Craps is an entertainment simulation game. We do not require account creation or personal login info. Game progress is saved locally on your device. We use Google AdMob to display rewarded video ads, which uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to handle user privacy choices and consent in applicable regions.

1. Information We Collect and Process

We do not collect or store personal identifying information such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical address.

  • Local Device Storage: The App stores game data (such as bankroll balance, game statistics, and roll history) locally on your device using standard web storage or local preference storage. This data never leaves your device.
  • Device Identifiers & Technical Data: When ads are served via Google AdMob, Google may collect non-personal device identifiers, IP addresses, and standard device telemetry to serve ads, prevent fraud, and measure ad performance.

2. Advertising & Google AdMob

Crapless Craps uses Google AdMob to offer optional rewarded video ads (allowing users to claim extra virtual game chips).

AdMob may use mobile advertising identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID) and cookies/similar technologies to display ads. Depending on your region, ads may be personalized or non-personalized.

3. User Messaging Platform (UMP) & GDPR / CCPA Consent

In compliance with the European Economic Area (EEA), UK, Switzerland, and US privacy regulations (including GDPR and CCPA/CPRA), Crapless Craps integrates the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK.

  • Consent Prompt: Users located in regions requiring consent are prompted with an official Google UMP consent form upon opening the app or requesting an ad.
  • Managing Your Privacy Choices: You may review or change your ad privacy preferences at any time from inside the app's Help & Settings menu ("Privacy & Ad Preferences" button).

4. Children's Privacy

Crapless Craps is intended for general audiences interested in craps table simulation. It does not offer real-money gambling, cash prizes, or real currency rewards. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the EU).

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be published directly on this page with an updated "Effective Date."
